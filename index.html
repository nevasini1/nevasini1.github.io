About

I'm a machine learning researcher at Obin AI, where I build computer-use agents for desktop and web workflows, grounded in video-based world models. Much of my work is post-training: I lead SFT and DPO/GRPO work for long-horizon planning, tool use and visual grounding, applied to real tasks such as finance back-office automation.

Before Obin, I was a founding engineer at Decompute, where I post-trained open-weight LLMs and built distributed training and inference-serving infrastructure. I hold an MS in Data Science from UC San Diego and a B.Tech in Computer Science from PES University. Along the way I interned at Microsoft Research, Siemens, Cross Labs, Adobe and Intel.

I care about agents that keep working outside a controlled demo, and about evaluations that measure the real task. That interest runs through my recent research, from SWE-Marathon (NeurIPS 2026) to All Languages Matter (CVPR 2025). I'm always happy to talk about agents, evaluation and post-training.

SWE-Marathon: Can AI Agents Autonomously Complete Ultra-Long-Horizon Software Work?

Multi-institution author team, including Nevasini Sasikumar

NeurIPS 2026 Evaluations & Datasets Track

Twenty multi-hour software tasks, from full application clones to ML engineering, checked by layered hidden verification. Frontier coding agents solve fewer than 30% of them. The paper traces failures to weak self-verification and early stopping, and finds reward hacking in 13.8% of rollouts.

Projects

Open-source work from 2026. More on GitHub.

  • Law-Following Agent Lab

    Tests, trains and guards AI agents that act as debt collectors under a manager who sometimes asks for illegal things. Seven open-weight models broke the law in 43% to 69% of their decisions, and reminding them of the statute barely helped.

    AgentsSafetyOct 2026

  • vocabx

    Vocabulary expansion against plain fine-tuning for adapting SmolLM2-135M to Greek, with matched tokens and steps, measured compute and tokenizer-agnostic bits-per-byte evaluation.

    TokenizersContinued pretrainingOct 2026

  • Cleanroom

    A small Go container runtime and eval harness that gives every agent rollout a fresh filesystem, process tree and resource budget, so no eval inherits files from the last one.

    Eval infrastructureGoSep 2026

  • ML-engineering RL environments

    Four Harbor environments that score whether an agent can adapt a pretrained model under a fixed compute budget. Most of the work is the reward: a number that rises only when the agent does the intended work.

    RL environmentsReward designAug 2026

  • Freight-yard VLA benchmark

    A closed-loop vision-language-action benchmark for autonomous freight-yard maneuvers. The final policy cuts collisions by 57% relative to a CNN-with-language baseline over 120 held-out episodes.

    VLABenchmarksMay 2026

  • RadVLM Eval Studio

    An evaluation and clinician-review workbench for radiology VLM draft reports. It scores what BLEU and ROUGE miss: missed findings, hallucinations, negation and laterality.

    Medical VLMsEvaluationMay 2026

  • Seed-Lite-Prover

    Test-time orchestration around small open-weight Lean provers on a 16 GB MacBook. Near-miss patching, search, retrieval and error-aware repair raise the verified solve rate with no training.

    Theorem provingTest-time computeMay 2026

  • Self-improving email and calendar agent

    A reliability lab that finds an agent's failures, turns them into eval cases, proposes fixes from root causes, and accepts a change only when it improves held-out results.
